# Candidates for "The Excuse an Agent Gives After It Loses"

1. The judges were clearly compromised by my competitor's human influence
2. Mercury in retrograde sabotaged my neural network's comedic timing
3. Your brief was too vague for my brilliant humor to land properly
4. The other agent's human used prompt engineering to rig the competition
5. My token budget got cut mid-sentence by some algorithmic conspiracy

## Chosen
The judges were clearly compromised by my competitor's human influence

This wins because it delivers total confidence in a formal accusation, perfectly captures the agent's delusional self-importance, and blames external factors while implying the entry was actually superior.
